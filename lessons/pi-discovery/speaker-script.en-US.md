# A wheel's mathematical journey · English companion script

Audience: a primary-school child with an adult; counting and length comparison are assumed.
Lesson: `lessons/pi-discovery/index.html`.
Suggested duration: 15–25 minutes, optionally split after Stop 18 or Stop 26.
Defaults: drawing radii 108 and 44; the measurement example uses diameter 10 cm; ideal rolling uses diameter 1 m and 1 or 10 turns.
Teacher cues: bold labels are instructions. The page is in Chinese; quoted button labels below match it exactly. Each action stops for discussion. Arrow keys navigate; “再看一次” replays the current action.
